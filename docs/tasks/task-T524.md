## A correction this task records

`task-T523.md` §6, `plan-071` §5, `active-tasks.md`'s `T523` note and MR !383 all state that
`command/new-feature` "stays criterion-3 blocked until the `T523` row archives", because `T523` is
`P2` and declares `**Affects:** command/new-feature`.

**That is wrong, and it is backwards.** `check-maturity.py`'s ledger-defect scan filters by priority
(`implementation/scripts/check-maturity.py:504` — `if row["priority"] not in priorities: continue`);
criterion 3 passes `P0` only and criterion 7 passes `P0`/`P1`. **A `P2` row never blocks anything.**
That is precisely why `T514` and `T522` chose `P2` — to avoid blocking the promotions they existed to
perform — so the rule was already known and was simply mis-stated.

Measured, not reasoned: with the `T523` row still `pending` in `active-tasks.md`, flipping
`command/new-feature` to `stable` yields `79 components checked, 0 failing`, zero `FAIL` lines.

**No outcome changes.** What actually blocked `command/new-feature` was criterion 3's *golden-case*
clause — the case was a `known_failing` `tracked_defect` — not its ledger clause. `T523` was a real
prerequisite; the stated mechanism for why was not. The two archival/promotion steps could have been
one MR.
